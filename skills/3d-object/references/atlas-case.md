# Atlas bridge faucet: scoped case notes

Use only when the user identifies the Atlas faucet from this workflow. Confirm the intended current variant before reuse. These notes are not a universal faucet template.

Source product page: https://vintaless.com/products/unlacquered-brass-bridge-faucet-lever-handles-atlas

Confirmed user measurements:

- Height above the mounting surface to the highest spout point: 17 inches / 431.8 mm. Concealed lower shanks are excluded.
- Mounting-centre spread: 8 inches / 203.2 mm. This is not outside width.
- Spout reach from vertical stem axis to outlet axis: 7 inches / 177.8 mm. This is not curved-tube length or outlet clearance.

The early 4-inch deck-to-handle interpretation produced a photographically incorrect compressed lower assembly and was superseded by later requests to correct leg and body proportions. Do not reuse 4 inches as a confirmed constraint for this visual model.

Observed target features: pale polished unlacquered brass; tall smoothly curved gooseneck; offset straight lever bars with longer outer arms; substantial valve housings and rounded bonnets; fuller cast bridge and integrated central shoulder; hexagonal unions, cylindrical legs, flat round escutcheons and stepped outlet.

The target handles are mirrored: the left extends farther left, the right farther right. The observed outer/inner ratio was approximately 2.2:1. Final draft values of 54/24 mm arms, 32.7 mm valve diameter, 28 mm riser diameter, 64.8 mm escutcheon diameter and 170 mm handle-underside elevation were photo-estimated, not manufacturer-confirmed.

The separate disassembled antique-copper faucet photograph was a reference for leg construction and lower threaded connections ONLY. Do not copy its angular spout, cross handles, spherical centre or copper finish. Draft 65 mm lower shanks, approximately 21.08 mm major diameter and 1.8 mm pitch were provisional visual values; no manufacturing thread standard was established.

The accepted draft was a usable review model, not a claim of an exact scan. Its material colour was estimated; later corrections must still be compared to the real original photograph. Public gallery/specification URLs can change; retrieve current evidence when using them. Do not claim access to private images or old local artifacts from this note alone.
