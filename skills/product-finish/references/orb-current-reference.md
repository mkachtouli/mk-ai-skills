# Current ORB direction — October 17, 2026

The user questioned the former golden/olive ORB result, requested manufacturer research, then requested a darker test and instructed updating the GitHub skill with the new finishes and references. Use [orb-dark-reference-2026-10-17.png](orb-dark-reference-2026-10-17.png) as the current visual direction. It is a generated, source-locked test selected for this skill update, not a photograph of a physical finish or a calibrated manufacturer sample.

- Base: deep chocolate/espresso brown; near-black in shadow, visibly warm brown on lit surfaces.
- Highlights: restrained copper-bronze warmth, localized on rounded exposed areas; no yellow-gold bands or artificial orange outlines.
- Surface: low satin sheen, fine rubbed texture and minimal visible brushing. Retain metallic depth without mirror gloss, flat black paint or coarse procedural patina.
- Keep all parts in one material family and preserve every original product detail. ORB must remain clearly darker and less golden than Antique Brass.

Names and appearances vary by maker. These sources support a darker test, not a universal exact RGB standard. Original physical ORB cap photographs remain useful for fine abrasion and changing sheen.
