## What to reproduce

- Deep, subdued red-brown copper with restrained warm copper highlights; the previous pale, polished faucet is too bright and reflective.
- Fine, dense directional abrasion visible close up, scaled to the target surface; avoid exaggerated scratch stripes, wood-like grain, or synthetic mottling.
- Low satin sheen: broad, soft reflections with a metallic response. Direct sun can produce a brighter edge or patch; do not flatten the finish into matte paint.
- Dark brown surfaces and recesses are part of the observed range. Preserve form and lighting variations, rather than painting every joint black or uniformly darkening the whole image.
- Violet/blue areas vary with outdoor reflections and viewing angle; treat their cause as uncertain without controlled lighting. Do not bake a uniform purple coating into the target.
- The gold ring and interior brass parts of the drain are separate components, not the AC base color. Hands, towel, railings, and background are not finish references.

## Review of future outputs

Compare the generated/edit result with both primary photographs for base darkness, fine grain scale, broad highlight width, and subdued reflection contrast. Reject mirror-like reflected shapes, bright rose-gold bands, orange/pink dominance, and coarse scratches. A fixed product outline alone does not demonstrate realistic material transfer.
