## Material and QA criteria

- Subdued reddish copper with brown depth; highlights can look gently pink-copper under the source light. Avoid exaggerated orange/salmon/rose-gold saturation or a uniform purple cast.
- Fine source-scaled abrasion, often lengthwise on tubes and following formed surfaces. No coarse procedural scratches, painted texture, rust-like blotches or hammered effect on smooth metal.
- Metallic satin-to-local restrained reflectivity. Keep broad soft volume shading and narrow highlights where lighting warrants them. Do not replace it with either uniform matte paint or smooth mirror-polished copper.
- Natural localized darker brown patina around existing joints, recesses and some rounded surfaces; do not invent black seams, outlines, patches or structural features.
- Different photographs have different lighting/exposure. Do not pick one RGB/hex value or claim a perfect colorimetric match.
- For a conversion, compare source geometry, full-product material consistency and 100% crops with the relevant example and physical drain texture. An unchanged silhouette or desaturated tint alone is not sufficient evidence of a realistic finish. Keep unresolved material issues as needs-review and do not upload as approved output.
