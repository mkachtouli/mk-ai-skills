## Current Oil-Rubbed Bronze reference set

The user supplied four additional photos on September 7, 2026 after finding the P-trap ORB results imperfect. Use these as the current material standard for `/ORB-FINISH` and ORB variants in `/ALL-FINISH`. They refine and take precedence over `orb-approved-reference.png`, the older showerhead reference retained for history. The request to strengthen the result means matching the richer warm metal tone and localized patina in these photos; it does not mean darkening the whole product.

Reference roles:

- [orb-reference-rounded-joints.png](orb-reference-rounded-joints.png) (user photo 2): primary base tone, fine hand-brushed texture, broad soft highlights, and satin reflectivity.
- [orb-reference-tubes-overall.png](orb-reference-tubes-overall.png) (user photo 4): primary consistency reference for long tubes, fittings, and plates across an entire product.
- [orb-reference-valve-detail.png](orb-reference-valve-detail.png) (user photo 1): supporting rounded surfaces, collars, worn highlights, and dark seams.
- [orb-reference-cylinder-detail.png](orb-reference-cylinder-detail.png) (user photo 3): supporting cylindrical parts and the darker end of the range on plates or shadowed areas. Do not use its darkest plate as the base tone for every surface.

View all four to understand the standard. Attach the two primary references to every ORB edit; include the supporting photos when relevant. Transfer only material color, fine grain, patina, and surface response, never the references' geometry, joints, marble background, or camera angle. Preserve the target photo's own light and shadows. Use the same primary pair for each source in a batch. A newer explicit user finish correction supersedes this set.

Material target and review criteria:

- Broad surfaces: medium-light muted golden bronze with olive-brown undertones; clearly warm and metallic. Reject gray/taupe dominance or an overall chocolate/black finish.
- Texture: fine, understated hand-brushed abrasion following the object's shape, with gentle natural variation. Reject coarse scratch bands, wood-like grain, heavy mottling, and invented corrosion.
- Sheen: soft satin highlights and softened reflections. Reject mirror-polished curves, bright yellow polished brass, or flat painted color.
- Patina: dark brown to near-black mainly inside existing seams, threaded recesses, and joints. Keep exposed tubes, nuts, caps, and plates in the same bronze family; do not add joints or artificial edge outlines to create contrast.

Compare the base tone, texture scale, sheen, and patina distribution against these references and against other images in the same batch. A reference-guided AI edit is a visual approximation, not a guaranteed colorimetric match. Do not label a merely generated image user-approved or claim perfect matching. PN and AC references are unaffected by this ORB revision.
