- [orb-reference-cylinder-detail.png](orb-reference-cylinder-detail.png) (user photo 3): supporting cylindrical parts and the darker end of the range on plates or shadowed areas. Do not use its darkest plate as the base tone for every surface.

Inspect these four only when historical comparison helps. Transfer only material color, fine grain, patina, and surface response, never their geometry, joints, marble background or camera angle. Preserve the target photo's light and shadows. The newer approved faucet image supersedes this set.

Material target and review criteria:

- Broad surfaces: medium-light muted golden bronze with olive-brown undertones; clearly warm and metallic. Reject gray/taupe dominance or an overall chocolate/black finish.
- Texture: fine, understated hand-brushed abrasion following the object's shape, with gentle natural variation. Reject coarse scratch bands, wood-like grain, heavy mottling, and invented corrosion.
- Sheen: soft satin highlights and softened reflections. Reject mirror-polished curves, bright yellow polished brass, or flat painted color.
- Patina: dark brown to near-black mainly inside existing seams, threaded recesses, and joints. Keep exposed tubes, nuts, caps, and plates in the same bronze family; do not add joints or artificial edge outlines to create contrast.

Compare the base tone, texture scale, sheen, and patina distribution against these references and against other images in the same batch. A reference-guided AI edit is a visual approximation, not a guaranteed colorimetric match. Do not label a merely generated image user-approved or claim perfect matching.
