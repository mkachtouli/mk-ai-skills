# Approved two-page print pattern

Use this as a starting composition, not a fixed arrangement when the product differs.

## Visual system

A4 portrait; white paper background; approximately 44 pt side margins; original logo in its own proportions; dark text, fine neutral table rules. Brand color can remain in the logo. Use a clear sans-serif for technical text, approximately 9 pt body, 11–13 pt section headings and a 16–18 pt main heading. Avoid decorative backgrounds that consume ink.

Header: brand logo on the left; product/model and short document descriptor on the right. Footer: brand/site and page count only. Never distort an image to fill a box. Use technical line drawings or background-removed product cutouts for technical figures. Photographic backdrops and broad background shadows must not print as grey rectangles.

## Page one: product and component references

- Product facts in two balanced columns. Put labels above short values when that uses the width better than a long, mostly empty full-width table. Common facts: material, handles, cartridge, mounting, connection, maximum countertop thickness and supported flow data. Omit fields that do not apply.
- Components drawing on the left and a bordered `REF. | COMPONENT | QTY` table on the right. Give text comfortable padding (roughly 5–7 pt horizontally and 4–6 pt vertically). Keep lines away from glyphs; do not squeeze additional rows by collapsing padding.
- Match every drawing callout to the table and check quantities, including paired parts and accessory groups. Numbers can repeat in a drawing for identical paired parts, but each reference has one table row.
- Keep the drawing readable but moderate in size. The approved Atlas example used approximately a 275 × 331 pt image box; that is a composition reference, not a product-size requirement. Prefer cropping excess white image margins or repositioning whole parts over changing their shapes.
- Available finishes can use the space below the parts table as a compact two-column list.
- Use a distinct lower section to compare package contents by configuration, e.g. Faucet Only vs With Sprayer. Do not imply that an optional accessory is included in every order.

## Page two: fit, installation and care

- Dimensions/mounting table on the LEFT; assembled dimensions illustration on the RIGHT. This reverses the image position from page one. Show supported overall height, reach, inlet spacing, mounting hole diameter and maximum deck thickness as applicable.
- Inches only by default, without decimal metric conversions. Keep fractional connection sizes legible. Do not round or convert a precise source into an unsupported fit claim. Never derive missing dimensions by measuring pixels. If a non-scale drawing conflicts visually with numeric labels, do not represent it as a drilling template.
- Below: a concise pre-installation check, numbered installation steps in one column, testing/care/troubleshooting in the other. Preserve the actual functional information when editing for space. Keep text natural and avoid duplicate passages.
- A separate support band near the bottom, separated by whitespace/rule. Contact instructions and QR belong together. Finish options belong with product/care information, not inside support.

## What to inspect at final scale

Readability of small callout numbers, diagram/table correspondence, row padding, consistent section gaps, no overlap with footer, no overflow/clipping, and balanced use of both pages. Use remaining space for supported useful information rather than enlarged decorative graphics or filler.
